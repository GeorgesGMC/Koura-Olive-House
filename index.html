Our Story

The Napolean family has grown olives in Bterram for three generations. Olives are picked by hand in October and November and pressed within 24 hours at a local press. The family started selling its oil in 1998 and now delivers across Lebanon.

New-season oil is available from 15 November 2026.

Delivery, Payment & Returns

Clear and straightforward policies across Lebanon.

Delivery Coverage & Fee

We deliver to all of Lebanon within 2–4 working days. Next-day delivery is available in Tripoli and Koura.

Delivery fee: $4 per order.
Free delivery on orders above $60.

Payment Options

We accept convenient local payment methods upon order placement or arrival:

  • Cash on delivery
  • Whish Money

Returns & Replacements

Returns: Unopened products can be returned within 7 days of delivery for a full refund.

Damaged items: A dented or leaking tin is replaced free of charge if reported on WhatsApp within 48 hours, with a photo.
